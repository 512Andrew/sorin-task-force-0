The operator behind the plan

Service shaped the standard.

Nick served more than two decades in the U.S. Army, advancing from enlisted Night Stalker to commissioned officer and leader. Across 14 years with the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment (Airborne), he completed 13 combat deployments and ultimately commanded Green Platoon—the regiment’s demanding assessment and selection pipeline.

That career forged more than grit. It built a disciplined way of thinking: understand the mission, prepare for what can go wrong, communicate without fog, and stay accountable until the objective is complete.

Today, Nick applies the same standard to financial strategy, leadership, education, and service—especially for veterans and military families navigating consequential decisions.
